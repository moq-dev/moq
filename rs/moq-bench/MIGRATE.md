This package has not been published. Use `--connect-backoff-*` and
`--connect-goaway-*` for reconnect and GOAWAY policy, and the corresponding
`MOQ_CONNECT_BACKOFF_*` and `MOQ_CONNECT_GOAWAY_*` variables. The earlier
unprefixed spellings stop startup and name their replacements.

# [M] Drop moq-transport 19-21

## Goal

`moq-net` and `@moq/net` no longer offer, accept, or contain code for
moq-transport drafts 19, 20, or 21. Gates shared by 18-19 collapse onto 18,
and the 20-22 family collapses onto 22.

## Plan

Decided 2026-10-24 with the maintainer; see the
[epic](/quest/m1/ietf-drop/README.md). No third-party peer is lost: imquic
and aiomoqt, the only speakers of 19 to 21, also speak 18 and 22. The
production savings are small (about 70 lines per language), but the test and
CI matrix drops three drafts, and each future IETF feature stops needing
cases for them.

- Rust: `ietf::Version` loses `Draft19`..`Draft21`, `version.rs` loses the
  `moqt-19`/`20`/`21` ALPNs and codes. Draft 21 is wire-identical to 20, and
  22 differs from 20 only in LOCATION_FILTER, so delete
  `draft21_matches_draft20_on_the_wire` and fold
  `draft22_changes_only_the_location_filter` into plain draft-22 tests.
  Collapse the 18-19 sets (PUBLISH_OK on listings, the GOAWAY request ID,
  `INVALID_JOINING_REQUEST_ID`, shared parameter sets in `publish.rs`,
  `fetch.rs`, and `error.rs`). Fuzz drops the three drafts.
- JS: `ietf/version.ts` loses `DRAFT_19`..`DRAFT_21`; fix its stale
  "unpublished" comment on draft 22, which datatracker published on
  2026-10-01.
- Public API: the `Version` variants and `moq-transport-19`..`21` names go
  (breaking, `!`). Wire: three ALPNs are no longer offered.
- CI: move the wasm lane (`test/wasm/run.sh`) and the drill lane off draft
  19, drop 20 from nightly (`test/interop/interop.sh`), drop every 19 to 21
  entry from `test/interop/interop.toml`, and fix fixtures such as
  `rs/moq-relay/tests/integration/cluster_unknown.rs`.
- Docs: the supported list in `doc/concept/standard.md`, and any quest that
  names 19 to 21 (such as the fill-timeout quest's "drafts 18-22").

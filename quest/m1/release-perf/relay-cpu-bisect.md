# [M] Find and fix the relay's per-frame CPU growth since [moq-dev/moq#5926](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/5926)

## Goal

The commits that raised the relay's instructions per delivered frame between [[cdff3c8f95](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/commit/cdff3c8f952b1d6bd2fd40c3f64eb0fd53b97e95)](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/commit/cdff3c8f95) ([moq-dev/moq#5926](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/5926) as merged) and `main` are found, each rise is attributed to a cause, and the avoidable costs are fixed. A rise that pays for a deliberate feature is accepted with its reason in the [release-perf epic](/quest/m1/release-perf/README.md).

## Plan

Found by [moq-dev/moq#7505](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/7505), which profiled the io_uring clock read. At `main` [`30f23c3f8a`](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/commit/30f23c3f8a), relay instructions per delivered frame had risen 25-56% on io_uring since [cdff3c8f95](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/commit/cdff3c8f952b1d6bd2fd40c3f64eb0fd53b97e95) and 18-38% on the tokio workers since `1dd9b83d1`, with the same delivery:

| workload | runtime | before, kinstr/frame | `30f23c3f8a`, kinstr/frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| chat | io_uring | 86.3 [84.5-87.4] at [cdff3c8f95](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/commit/cdff3c8f952b1d6bd2fd40c3f64eb0fd53b97e95) | 134.8 [128.5-151.3] |
| video | io_uring | 108.7 [106.6-116.2] at [cdff3c8f95](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/commit/cdff3c8f952b1d6bd2fd40c3f64eb0fd53b97e95) | 136.1 [132.6-152.8] |
| chat | tokio workers | 104.0 [101.4-105.0] at `1dd9b83d1` | 143.1 [127.5-205.3] |
| video | tokio workers | 141.6 [139.3-148.3] at `1dd9b83d1` | 167.1 [164.9-173.6] |

Medians of 5 interleaved reps, min-max in brackets. The tokio rows start at [`1dd9b83d1`](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/commit/1dd9b83d19), [moq-dev/moq#5926](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/5926)'s base, since [moq-dev/moq#7505](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/7505) measured the tokio workers only there and at `main`. Both runtimes moved, so at least one cause is shared code (moq-net's model, kio, or the session layer). That does not rule out a further io_uring-only cost, such as the clock read [moq-dev/moq#7505](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/7505) profiled. The tokio range also spans [moq-dev/moq#5926](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/5926) itself, so the two rises can't be subtracted to find one.

Decided 2026-11-01 (maintainer, follow-up planning after the 2026-11-01 /quest-afk run): m1, in this epic, since `main` must not reach the cut carrying accidental per-frame cost.

- Measure as [moq-dev/moq#7505](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/7505) did: `--profile profiling` builds with frame pointers and `moq-relay/io-uring,moq-bench/uring`, one target directory per revision, `perf stat` `cpu_core` instructions (user and kernel) over the steady window, divided by the frames the generator's 500 ms reports count over the same window. 4 io_uring workers, not 1, so chat does not saturate a worker. Pin the relay and generator cores so a loaded host still reads instruction deltas.
- Bisect `cdff3c8f95..main` on chat over io_uring first (the largest rise and the tightest ranges), with a step size that keeps each build's cost bounded, then confirm the culprits on video and on the tokio workers. A rise that shows on io_uring but not on the tokio workers is attributed on its own, not folded into a shared culprit.
- Attribute each step with `perf record` at a fixed period, as [moq-dev/moq#7505](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/7505) did, before deciding fix or accept. Record accepted costs in the epic's Plan with the feature they pay for.
- Commit the driver this needs as a deterministic mode of `just bench-profile` or `just bench-runtime` (pinned cores, instructions per delivered frame, fixed-period samples) instead of another throwaway script; [moq-dev/moq#7505](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/7505)'s driver on `bench/relay.sh` was not committed.
- Checkpointed at the maintainer's stop request in [moq-dev/moq#7605](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/7605), without a production fix or completed attribution. The [measurement report](/.github/relay-cpu-bisect.md) and [per-cell evidence](/.github/relay-cpu-bisect.json) retain counts, matching delivery boundaries, source sample markers, artifact hashes, and excluded attempts. Raw captures remain in that checkout's `.tmp/bench/`.
- `just bench-profile instructions` now provides disjoint relay/generator CPU sets, acknowledged PMU windows, delivery interpolated from surrounding 500 ms reports, alternating revision pairs, isolated revision builds, and fixed-period instruction stacks. It refuses unavailable/multiplexed counters and lost/throttled samples. Historical profiling checkouts live under `.tmp/` and load only their own Cargo configuration.
- The host's original 1,000 Hz sample-rate limit throttled every sample. Gated measurements temporarily requested 100,000 Hz and restored the original on exit; the driver changes no sysctls. A parser defect discovered during collection is fixed: identical cumulative snapshots at one millisecond remain valid, while contradictory observations are refused. Failed attempts are excluded explicitly.
- Completed exploratory chat comparisons use the same [21d6da9fc1](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/commit/21d6da9fc108b48373a961f34e43c3fcd38b0146) production control, four io_uring workers, cores 4-7 for the relay and 0-3 for the generator, three alternating pairs, and a fixed period of 10,000,019 instructions. The old endpoint's median is 108.7k instructions/frame [108.0-118.3k], versus its paired current control's 156.8k [156.0-157.7k], at approximately 20,000 frames/s with zero confirmed loss in the boundary reports. No outlier cells were trimmed. These are sampled delivery estimates on a shared host, not confidence intervals.
- Attribution remains partial. The range-serving boundary [moq-dev/moq#5929](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/5929) accounts for a smaller measured historical step. [moq-dev/moq#6224](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/6224) introduced repeated `Reader::learn_end` polling; its after-change capture is retained but inconclusive because delivery changed and confirmed losses were reported. The source introduction is verified; its instruction effect is not yet confirmed. Later [moq-dev/moq#7532](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/7532) watch changes already reduce that path on the current control. Current `poll_forward_drop` stacks remain prominent and overlap the separately owned [moq-dev/moq#7493](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/7493); those production files were not edited here. No feature cost has been accepted in the epic from these incomplete measurements.
- Remaining: obtain a comparable-delivery measurement of the resume-end boundary, then attribute the growth between [fe722cec9f](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/commit/fe722cec9f8432e0e46cb633cc5e79e59e7724cb) and the parent of [fd59eb18a5](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/commit/fd59eb18a50f6e0ac819e73d941a3832cbdac91f), then account for changes through the pre-dynamic point [55f6ed2292](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/commit/55f6ed229250d17fc99389237659906d011d8a94) and the pinned current control [21d6da9fc1](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/commit/21d6da9fc108b48373a961f34e43c3fcd38b0146), including improvements that offset rises. Coordinate track/resume/publisher changes with [moq-dev/moq#7493](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/pull/7493). Confirm attributed steps on video and Tokio workers, fix remaining avoidable costs with publisher/viewer benchmark sweeps, record justified feature-cost acceptances in the epic, and complete local review. None of those remaining experiments or fixes was started after the stop request.
- Validation: focused measurement tests and lints pass, but two full local gates failed on the known mbx E2BIG and runner namespace denial; an unchanged JS test also failed on the first attempt and passed on the second. The PR remains a draft and other-vendor review has not run. Preserve those limits when resuming.
- [Fanout lock contention](/quest/m1/release-perf/fanout-lock.md) covers the bimodal futex cost on the fanout workload; don't fix it twice. A bisect step that lands on the same lock goes there.

Public API: none expected. Wire: none.
